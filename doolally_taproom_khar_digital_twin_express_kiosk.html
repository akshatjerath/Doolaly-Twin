Assumptions & Evidence Log — Doolally Taproom Khar

1. Verified Primary & Secondary Evidence (Real-World Anchor)

  • Spatial Layout (6th Floor, Multilink House, Khar West): Derived from video walkthroughs—rectangular rooftop terrace with a sloped white canopy roof, staircase entry opening onto the floor, left-side enclosed kitchen with glass viewing windows and long bar counter, pillar-mounted chalkboard tap menus, long wooden communal tables on the right, and a pull-down projector screen on the far wall.
  • Official Craft Styles & Benchmarks: Doolally's official tap styles include Apple Cider, Alphonso Mead, Belgian Witbier, Oatmeal Stout, Hefeweizen, and IPA/NEIPA; video walkthroughs also verify seasonal Strawberry Mead and 30–50ml tasting glasses. Zomato Khar benchmarks a pint at ~₹330 and ₹1,500 for two.
  • Food & Mug Club Evidence: Starter items (e.g., Bhuna Kathal Quesadillas, Prawn Koliwada) are anchored in Doolally Mumbai listings; Mug Club's "50% more with every pour" perk is verified on Doolally's official Mug Club page.
  • Maharashtra State Excise Regulatory Boundary: Under Maharashtra FL-III rules, on-premises liquor consumption is strictly restricted to the pre-approved, plan-marked permit room area (consuming liquor in public outside licensed premises is prohibited). Sealed off-consumption takeaway beer requires a separate FL/BR-II retail licence; therefore, the proposed Express Kiosk operates strictly as an on-premises FL-III Wait Lounge inside the licensed rooftop boundary.

2. Simulated Discrete-Event Queue & Digital Twin Assumptions

  • Seating Topology: Modeled as 6 communal wooden tables (8 seats each = 48 seats) + 8 bar stools = 56 total capacity. In Scenarios 1 & 2, parties occupy whole communal table sections (leaving stranded seats); Scenario 3 enables seat-level communal matching (+24% effective capacity utilization).
  • Balking & Reneging Thresholds: Based on restaurant queueing literature (DES models), unengaged guests waiting on stairs (Scenario 1) exhibit a sharp reneging curve once estimated wait exceeds 25 minutes. Engaging waiting guests in the FL-III Wait Lounge with 150ml tasters (₹165–₹190) and quick bites increases wait tolerance to ~55 minutes and converts 68–74% of waiting parties into pre-seating revenue.
